Deploy a specialized car damage detection model to automate insurance claims processing and vehicle inspections. This project features a pre-trained YOLOv8 model and a 3.2k-image dataset annotated to identify damage on critical components like bumpers, doors, fenders, and windshields, providing a robust foundation for AI-driven auto repair estimates.

## Ways to Use Car Damage Detection Model

1. **Insurance Claims Processing**: This model could be used by insurance companies to automate the process of accident reports and insurance claims. Users could submit pictures of their damaged cars, and the model would help identify the damaged parts, thus speeding up the claims-processing time.

2. **Auto Repair Estimates**: Auto repair shops could use the model to provide online estimates for car repairs. Customers could upload images of their damaged cars, and the model could identify the damage, aiding in determining the cost of repair.

3. **Car Resale Platforms**: Online platforms for buying and selling used cars could implement this model. It would help in assessing the condition of cars being sold, providing a more accurate value and informing potential buyers about any damage.

4. **Automatic Vehicle Inspection**: The model could be deployed in vehicle inspection stations. It could help automatically identify any damage, saving time and increasing the efficiency of inspection processes.

5. **Parking Lot Monitoring**: The model could be integrated into security cameras at parking lots. It would aid in detecting any damage done to parked cars, helping to identify instances of collisions or vandalism swiftly.
